OpenAI has banned a coordinated network of ChatGPT accounts linked to a Cambodia-based fraud operation in Poipet, which was using generative AI to power investment scams, romance fraud, gambling schemes, and impersonation of law enforcement. The operation is believed to originate from Southeast Asia, a region increasingly associated with large-scale, technology-enabled fraud compounds. This highlights the growing abuse of legitimate AI platforms to automate and scale social engineering attacks.


> **Security Architect's Take:** Review your organisation's acceptable use and data loss prevention policies to ensure employees are not engaging with unsolicited AI-assisted communications; consider threat intelligence feeds that flag AI-generated phishing and social engineering campaigns so your security awareness training reflects this evolving attack vector.
