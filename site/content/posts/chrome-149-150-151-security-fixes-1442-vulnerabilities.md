Google has fixed 1,442 security vulnerabilities across three recent Chrome releases (versions 149, 150, and 151), more than the previous 23 updates combined. The sheer volume is unprecedented and suggests a significant shift in Google's vulnerability discovery or disclosure process, possibly driven by increased internal tooling or AI-assisted fuzzing. While no specific critical zero-days are detailed in the summary, the scale of patching makes prompt updates essential.


> **Security Architect's Take:** Ensure Chrome is enforced at the latest stable version across all managed endpoints via your MDM or browser management policy — with 1,442 flaws resolved in quick succession, any unpatched instance represents meaningful attack surface. Prioritise validation that auto-update policies are functioning and consider auditing browser version compliance reporting in your cloud-connected endpoints.
