China's government has launched an investigation into the security of Palo Alto Networks' products, without publicly stating its reasons. This mirrors a similar probe against Micron in 2023, which ultimately resulted in a ban on Micron products in critical infrastructure. The move is widely interpreted as geopolitical leverage rather than a genuine security concern, but it signals potential supply chain and procurement risk for organisations operating in or with China.


> **Security Architect's Take:** Organisations with operations in China that rely on Palo Alto Networks firewalls, Prisma Cloud, or other PAN products should begin mapping their exposure and review contingency plans for potential procurement restrictions or forced replacement. Engage with your legal and compliance teams now rather than waiting for an official outcome.
