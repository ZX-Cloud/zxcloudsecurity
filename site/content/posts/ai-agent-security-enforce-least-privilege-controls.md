As AI agents become embedded in cloud environments, security teams are discovering that simply monitoring what agents are doing is insufficient — enforcing strict controls over what they are permitted to do is the real challenge. Implementing least-privilege principles for AI agents is proving far more complex than traditional workload identity, due to the dynamic, intent-driven nature of agent actions. A range of emerging approaches, from prompt filtering to identity-layer access controls, are being explored but no single standard has emerged.


> **Security Architect's Take:** Begin auditing your AI agent deployments now to map the permissions and data access each agent holds, then apply identity-layer controls and scope restrictions as tightly as possible — treat AI agents as non-human identities subject to the same (or stricter) IAM governance as service accounts.
