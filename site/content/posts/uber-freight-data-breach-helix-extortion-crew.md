A threat actor known as Helix has claimed responsibility for breaching Uber Freight, alleging the theft of nearly one million files. Uber Freight states that logistics operations were not disrupted, though the extortion claim suggests sensitive data may be at risk. This incident highlights the growing targeting of logistics and supply chain platforms by extortion-focused criminal groups.


> **Security Architect's Take:** Review your data classification and exfiltration detection controls — particularly egress monitoring and DLP policies on file stores. Ensure third-party logistics platforms and partner integrations are included in your threat modelling and have defined breach notification SLAs.
