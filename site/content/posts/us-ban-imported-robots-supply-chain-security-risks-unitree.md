The United States has moved to ban the import of certain foreign-manufactured robots, citing supply chain integrity and national security concerns. Chinese robotics firm Unitree is highlighted as a key example of the perceived threat, with fears that embedded hardware or software could facilitate espionage or sabotage. This follows a broader pattern of US regulatory action targeting technology with Chinese ties, and has implications for organisations procuring physical automation systems.


> **Security Architect's Take:** Cloud security architects overseeing OT/IoT or physical security integrations should audit any robotic or automation hardware in their estate for foreign-manufactured components, particularly those with network connectivity or cloud management planes — ensure vendor risk assessments explicitly cover firmware provenance, telemetry data flows, and supply chain transparency before procurement or deployment.
